# Partnership Terms

This document records the ownership and operating agreement between the
partners for this repository and its codebase, as of 2026-08-20.

## Ownership

- The codebase in this repository is **joint intellectual property** of
  both partners (Camilo Daza and Oskar Bal).
- Each partner holds a **perpetual license** to use, modify, and deploy the
  code as it exists at any point in time — this license survives even if
  the partnership or either partner's contribution to the project ends.

## Territory and revenue

- Oskar deploys and sells this product to clients in **Poland**.
- Camilo deploys and sells this product to clients in the **United States**.
- Neither partner owes the other a cut or commission on deals closed in
  their respective territory.

## Codebase structure

- Improvements to the **core engine** (shared functionality not specific to
  a single client or vertical) are pushed back to the shared `main` branch
  so both partners benefit.
- **Client-specific work** (branding, custom scoring rules, integrations
  built for a particular deployment) stays private to the partner who built
  it and is not required to be merged upstream.

## Costs

- Any costs associated with deploying this product (hosting, database,
  third-party vendors, etc.) will be decided **before** anything is
  deployed to production. No cost-sharing arrangement is assumed yet.

## If a partner stops contributing

- If one partner stops contributing to the project, the other partner
  **retains full rights** to the codebase under the joint-ownership terms
  above.
