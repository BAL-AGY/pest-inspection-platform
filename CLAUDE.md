## Project overview

We are building a production-ready **customer acquisition platform** for a
pest control company. This is not an e-commerce/checkout-first product.

The primary business objective is the following funnel:

```
TRAFFIC / OUTREACH
  → AWARENESS
  → LEAD CAPTURE
  → PROSPECT
  → MARKETING QUALIFIED LEAD (MQL)
  → SALES QUALIFIED LEAD (SQL)
  → BOOKED FREE HOME INSPECTION
  → INSPECTION
  → CUSTOMER WON / LOST
```

**The most important conversion in this entire platform is a QUALIFIED,
BOOKED FREE HOME INSPECTION** — not an online purchase of pest control
services. The pest control company wants these appointments so its owner or
technician can get their "foot in the door," inspect the property in person,
and attempt to close the homeowner into a pest control agreement. Every
design and product decision should optimize for getting a qualified lead to a
booked, kept inspection appointment.

The active north-star metric for this build is **cost per qualified booked
inspection** (see the session goal / `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` for the full
scope and end-to-end definition of done).
